Why the universe obeys equations

The physicist Eugene Wigner called the usefulness of mathematics in physics unreasonable and said there is no rational explanation for it (Wigner, 1960). HMR offers one.

In HMR, mathematics is the ordered pattern of thought within a Universal Intelligence (UI), existing among thoughts without order. Nature is mathematical because it is made of that ordered thought.

The starting premise

A Universal Intelligence exists. Before the Big Bang it was unlimited. The Big Bang is the manifestation of a system in which that Universal Intelligence imposes limits on itself.

Every claim that follows is tied to something that can be tested.

The God question

How did an unlimited Universal Intelligence create a reality that limits it? Explain and define the mechanics.

HMR has not solved it. The mathematics on the full page is the attempt to build an answer, starting from thoughts and the act of thinking.

Patterns and proofs

A pattern is a thought that, repeated, gives the same result. A proof is a path of thought from one ordered statement to another that never leaves order. That is why a proof, once found, holds for everyone.

The mechanics of thought

If reality is UI thinking, there should be a mathematics whose only object is a thought and whose only operation is thinking one. The full page builds it from six assumptions, and every line carries a label that says how firm it is.

  • One intelligence. Reality is the thinking of one Universal Intelligence.
  • One element. The element is a thought. A thought has no place and no size. All it has is how it relates to other thoughts.
  • One relation. Thoughts happen one at a time, and each new thought builds on some of the thoughts before it. The relation never runs in a circle.
  • Only the pattern counts. If two thoughts are unrelated, it makes no difference which of them came first.
  • No reaching across. What is thought next depends on what it builds on, and not on thoughts unrelated to it.
  • Liking. Every possible next thought has some chance, and the chances are UI's liking: it does more of what it likes.

The six slots, and how far along each is

A model of reality has to fill six slots: matter, space, gravity, energy, time, and thought. Four more slots are listed because the work needed them. Scores run from 0 (nothing) to 5 (derived and tested against measurement). The scores are our own judgment, not a measurement.

Where Version 2 stands

Version 2 is dated November 23, 2026. Version 1 was dated November 20, 2026.

What was wrong with Version 1

It described thoughts and how they pile up, not thinking. Nothing in it could be true, false, or a lie, and it had no emotion and no memory. It also read one rule of steady growth as a candidate for inflation without testing whether what grows is a space.

What Version 2 adds

Definitions of truth, mistakes, lies, and emotion. An exact answer to what one false part does to an idea. Proofs checked by computer on every small pattern. Two negative results and one positive: rules that copy shapes do remember.

Open

Where we are falling short

How a thought comes to be about another is still open. The definitions are proved for single thoughts, not for human minds. No rule yet grows space, holds a memory in one place, or makes a particle. We predict no number that physics lacks, and we have made no progress on any famous open problem.

Where AI takes over

Our hypothesis is that once a human idea is complete enough, it becomes the context from which AI can produce the mathematics and physics. With the help of computer scientists, what is written here can be made rigorously mathematical, given back to AI, and sharpened round after round.
